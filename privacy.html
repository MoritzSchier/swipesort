Privacy Policy – SwipeSort

Last updated: November 2026

SwipeSort helps you organize the photo library on your iPhone. SwipeSort does not collect any personal data.

What data does the app process?

  • Photos and videos: With your permission, SwipeSort accesses your photo library to display items, add them to albums, mark them as favorites, hide them or – after you confirm in the iOS dialog – delete them. Your photos stay on your device or in your iCloud Photos. They are not sent to us or to third parties by the app.
  • Album suggestions: Suggestions are calculated only on your iPhone (Apple Vision). The calculated image features are stored locally in the app.
  • Place names and maps: To show places such as “Berlin”, the app sends only the GPS coordinates of a photo to Apple’s map service (Apple Maps / CLGeocoder, MapKit). Apple’s privacy policy applies. No photos are transmitted for this.
  • Settings and progress: Which items you have sorted, your gestures and statistics are stored only on your device.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No tracking, ads or analytics tools
  • No sharing or selling of data

Deleting your data

Deleting SwipeSort removes all data stored by the app. Your photos remain in the Photos app.
