FAQ

Where are deleted photos?
They stay in the Photos app under “Recently Deleted” for 30 days and can be recovered there.

Why isn’t storage freed immediately?
iOS frees storage once “Recently Deleted” is emptied: Photos → Recently Deleted → Select → Delete All.

Are my photos uploaded?
No. SwipeSort processes everything on your iPhone.

How do I change swipe directions?
Gear icon at the top right → Swipe Directions.
